Who We Are

Built by Women, for Community

Kyankima Women's Cooperative was founded in 2019 by 47 women in Mukono District, Uganda, who shared a vision: that collective effort could transform small-scale farming and handcraft production into a sustainable livelihood.

What began as informal market days and word-of-mouth trade has grown into a registered community-based organisation. Our members grow vegetables, fruits, and grains; weave and craft baskets, mats, and textiles; and work alongside health professionals to bring practical wellness knowledge to our communities.

We are now proudly online — connecting our products and knowledge to customers across Uganda and beyond.

"We started with nothing but our hands and our trust in each other. Today we sell to Kampala, and tomorrow — the world."

— Nalwoga Judith, Chairperson

Monthly Health Tips

Wellness from Our Community

Our three partner health workers share practical, locally relevant health advice every month.

🥗
Sr. Nakato Aisha · Community Nurse

Eating Local for Better Nutrition

Nakati, sweet potato leaves, and groundnuts provide more iron and protein than many imported supplements. Cook with minimal water to preserve nutrients. A balanced local meal is often more nutritious than expensive packaged foods.

💧
Mr. Wasswa David · Environmental Health Officer

Safe Water Practices at Home

Boil drinking water for at least one minute or use a locally made ceramic filter. Store water in a covered, clean container. Wash hands with soap before cooking and after using the latrine — these two habits prevent most waterborne illnesses.

👶
Ms. Namukasa Rehema · Midwife

Maternal Health During Pregnancy

Visit an antenatal clinic at least four times during pregnancy. Eat iron-rich foods like beans, dark greens, and groundnuts daily. Sleep under a treated mosquito net every night — malaria is the leading complication for pregnant women in Uganda.
